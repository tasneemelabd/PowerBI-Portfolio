# Olist E-commerce Dashboard 📊

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing e-commerce data from **Olist**, a Brazilian marketplace platform. The dashboard turns raw sales, customer, and product data into clear insights that support data-driven business decisions.

## 🔍 Overview

The dashboard is organized into 4 main pages:

- **Executive Dashboard** – A high-level summary of total revenue, total orders, total products, and average review score, with monthly revenue trends and order status breakdown.
- **Sales Analysis** – Deep dive into sales performance by product category and geographic region (customer state/city).
- **Customer Experience** – Analysis of customer behavior, including total vs. repeat customers and their geographic distribution across Brazil.
- **Product Performance** – Product-level insights on total items sold, average review scores, and revenue by category.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Power BI
- DAX (calculated measures & columns)
- Data Modeling
- Data Visualization

## 📈 Key Insights

- Identified top-performing product categories by revenue (e.g., health & beauty, watches & gifts, bed/bath/table).
- Tracked monthly revenue trends to highlight seasonal peaks and drops.
- Measured customer retention through repeat customer rate.
- Mapped customer distribution geographically across Brazilian states.
